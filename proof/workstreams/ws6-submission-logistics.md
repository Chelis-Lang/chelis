# WS6: Submission Logistics

- **Size:** Small (thin tail).
- **Dependencies:** [WS4](ws4-paper-draft.md), [WS5](ws5-supplementary.md). Anonymization checklist lives separately at [../anonymization.md](../anonymization.md).
- **Output:** Submitted POPL 2027 paper on popl27.hotcrp.com.

---

## WS6.1 — TPMS registration

At least one senior author registers on torontopapermatching.org, uploads 5–10 representative papers. Must be done before submission. Failure risks desk rejection.

## WS6.2 — HotCRP setup

Register on popl27.hotcrp.com. Enter author info, conflicts, topics.

## WS6.3 — Anonymization pass

Run through [`../anonymization.md`](../anonymization.md). Specifically:

- No author names or affiliations in paper or supplementary material
- Own work referenced in third person
- No acknowledgments
- Lean code: no names, no institutional paths, no git history, no identifiable URLs
- BibTeX: own publications anonymized
- Supplementary PDF: clean metadata

## WS6.4 — Final checks

Page count ≤ 25 (excl. bibliography). Correct `acmart` format. PDF printable on US Letter and A4. All appendices in supplementary material, not main PDF.
