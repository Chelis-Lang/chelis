`Std.Decimal` now computes exactly instead of failing on every call. A `Decimal` is the rational `c / 10^s` with at most 38 significant digits and at most 38 fractional digits, held in one canonical form, so `eq` and `neq` compare the numbers themselves and `decimal_lt`, `decimal_lte`, `decimal_gt`, and `decimal_gte` order them. `decimal` parses an RFC 8259 number and `decimal_to_string` writes the canonical text. `decimal_add`, `decimal_sub`, and `decimal_mul` are exact and fail rather than round. `decimal_round`, `decimal_div`, `decimal_to_i64`, and `decimal_from_f64` take a `Rounding` from `Std.Rounding`. `decimal_to_fixed_string` pads without rounding, `decimal_to_f64` and `decimal_to_f32` round once to nearest with ties to even, and `decimal_scale` returns the canonical scale. `try_decimal_div`, `try_decimal_from_f64`, and `try_decimal_to_i64` return `None` where their twins fail `domain`, and `try_decimal_to_i64` also where its twin fails `overflow`. Every failure reports `<function>: domain: <detail>` or `<function>: overflow: <detail>`, and no primitive numeric trap escapes a call. `Decimal` is opaque: build one with `decimal`, `decimal_from_i64`, or `decimal_from_f64`; record literals, field access and constructor patterns outside the module are rejected, and the public `coefficient` and `scale` fields are gone. `RoundingMode` and `round_half_up`, `round_half_even`, `round_down`, and `round_up` are removed in favor of `Std.Rounding`, `decimal_from_int` in favor of `decimal_from_i64`, `decimal_to_float` in favor of `decimal_to_f64`, and `decimal_eq` in favor of `eq`. See [#2778](https://github.com/Chelis-Lang/chelis/issues/2778).
