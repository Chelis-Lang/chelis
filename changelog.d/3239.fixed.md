A macro call must supply exactly one argument per macro parameter, and a macro definition may not repeat a parameter name. `chelis check`, `eval`, and `build` now reject a call with too few or too many arguments, naming the macro and both counts, and reject a repeated parameter, naming the macro and the parameter. Previously a missing argument let its parameter resolve to a same-named binding at the call site, a surplus argument was dropped before name, type, effect, and linearity checking, and a repeated parameter silently took the last argument. This applies to user-defined and standard prelude macros. See [#3239](https://github.com/Chelis-Lang/chelis/issues/3239).
