`chelis prove --tier fuzz-only` now opens every scheduled binder's
off-diagonal cell window at one boundary shared by the property rather than at
each binder's own schedule end, so binders whose schedule lengths differ still
pair. A per-binder window was as wide as the cell count and so disjoint
between binders whose lengths differed by that much — two `!=` exclusions on
one binder lost every off-diagonal pairing at any budget (chelis#3697).
