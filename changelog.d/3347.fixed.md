A declared extent is now checked when the tensor it claims sits inside a
tuple, an `Option`, a `List`, a record or another nominal value, including one
a nominal dimension argument supplies: `-> Box[3]` with
`Box[n] = Box { v: tensor[n, f32] }` claims axis 0 of `v`. Results, local
ascriptions and formals trap `Domain` at the nested tensor's producer, or at
`load` on entry, on both `chelis eval` and compiled C, where the claim was
previously dropped and a disagreeing extent ran to completion. A claim that
passes through a recursive type whose arguments grow without repeating is
refused with a diagnostic. See
[#3347](https://github.com/Chelis-Lang/chelis/issues/3347) and
[#2644](https://github.com/Chelis-Lang/chelis/issues/2644).
