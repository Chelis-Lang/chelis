`mod`, `bitand`, `bitor`, `bitxor`, `shl`, `shr`, `cast` and `cast_trunc` now accept a borrowed tensor operand, such as `mod(&x, &y)` or `cast(&x, f64)`, and leave the borrowed values readable, as the arithmetic operations already do. Before, the checker refused these with a dtype message such as "requires matching integer arguments" even when the dtypes matched, and a borrowed cast source failed with "borrow is only valid as a direct call argument". An owned operand to the integer operations is now borrowed rather than consumed. See [#3101](https://github.com/Chelis-Lang/chelis/issues/3101).
