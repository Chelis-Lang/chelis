New generic wrappers may no longer infer hidden `len`, `index`, `append`, or `concat` admission predicates from their bodies: they must declare a sufficient `List` or `Dict` contract. Already-checked builtin function values retain their precise collection operand/result relations through aliases, higher-order and recursive calls, aggregates, imports, and serialized TypeEnv checker reuse, so invalid indirect scalar or mismatched-list calls are rejected. See [#1654](https://github.com/Chelis-Lang/chelis/issues/1654).
