`Std.Datetime` replaces `Std.Time` in `chelis-std`. It provides opaque, validated civil dates, times, datetimes, instants on the POSIX timescale, fixed offsets, offset datetimes, exact durations, calendar periods, and date and instant columns over years -9999 through 9999, with RFC 3339-based text forms and required policies for month-end overflow and rounding. The new `Std.Rounding` module defines the shared rounding modes, `RoundTowardNegative` through `RejectInexact`. Every failure reports `<function>: domain: <detail>` for an input that denotes no value, or `<function>: overflow: <detail>` for arithmetic that leaves its type, and no primitive numeric trap escapes a call. `Std.Time`, whose callables all failed, is removed, and importing it fails. Its `Date`, `Duration`, and `DayOfWeek` have no field-compatible successor: build dates with `Std.Datetime.date` or `parse_date`, durations with `duration` or `duration_from_count`, and use `Weekday`. See [#2859](https://github.com/Chelis-Lang/chelis/issues/2859).
