A `shape(...)` read that reaches an `expand` or `insert` size through a record field (`shape(inputs.features, 0i32)`) or through a pipe (`x |> shape(cast(0, int32)) |> cast(int64)`) is now an admissible runtime extent, and executes on both the evaluator and the C backend. Multi-input models that take their arguments as a record no longer need a prologue local before each shape read, and `chelis lint --fix`'s `prefer-pipe-operator` rewrite no longer turns a building program into a rejected one (chelis#1266, chelis#569).
