`chelis cost` no longer runs out of memory on a recursion that calls itself in both arms of a branch whose condition is known only at runtime, such as a bisection root finder. Tensor-DAG lowering now inlines at most 32,768 function bodies per entry; an entry past that budget is rejected with a diagnostic that names the recursion, and `cost` and `build` fall back to the host lane as they do for the existing unroll limits.
